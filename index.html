Frequently asked questions

Questions? We have answers.

Find quick answers about our services, projects, pricing and communication.

What services does K Digital Hub offer?

We offer website development, branding and logo design, posters and digital designs, typing and document services, digital business setup and custom digital projects.

How much does a website cost?

Website pricing depends on the number of pages, features, design requirements and overall project scope. Contact us for a quotation based on your needs.

How do I start a project?

Complete the project enquiry form and send your requirements. We will receive your message and can discuss the project, requirements and next steps with you.

How long does a project take?

Delivery time depends on the type and size of the project. A timeline will be discussed after we understand your requirements.

Can you design a logo or poster for my business?

Yes. We can create professional logos, posters, social media graphics and other digital designs according to your business or event requirements.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. Project discussions, files and updates can be handled digitally, making it possible to work with clients from different locations.
